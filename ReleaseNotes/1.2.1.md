Join now opens the actual meeting link. Invites often contain several links to the same provider — Microsoft Teams ones in particular carry "Meeting options", dial-in, channel and app links alongside the real one — and Heads Up could pick whichever appeared first in the invite instead of the link that actually joins the meeting. It now works out which one is the join link. The same applies to Zoom and Webex invites that include download or dial-in links.
