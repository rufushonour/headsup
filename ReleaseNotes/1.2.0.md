The full-screen alert can no longer get stuck. Press Esc to dismiss it, or Return to join when there's a single meeting to join.

Connecting or disconnecting a display while an alert is up now works properly too — the alert redraws itself for the displays you actually have. Previously, unplugging a screen could leave the alert stranded with its buttons off-screen, and because it covers the menu bar there was no way to close it short of force-quitting.

The tray menu now also shows which version you're running, just above Check for Updates.
